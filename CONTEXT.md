# Pid Sklom

A Ukrainian-art meme project connecting a phrase or caption to a painting and its
reviewed historical context.

## Language

**Artist**:
The creator of an artwork, with an identity and biography independent of any one
painting.
_Avoid_: Author, when referring to the creator of a painting

**Artwork**:
A source work of art with its artist, title, provenance and image. It is not the
captioned image created by a visitor.
_Avoid_: Meme, when referring to the original painting

**Meme profile**:
A description of an artwork's visible content, mood and possible humorous uses,
including uses to avoid. It is interpretive material, not verified art history.
_Avoid_: Image explanation database

**Meme situation**:
A short everyday scenario that an artwork can illustrate, used to connect a
visitor's phrase to a suitable painting.

**Fact**:
A sourced historical statement about an artist or artwork. A generated statement
does not become a verified fact without review.

**Meme**:
A visitor's caption and visual treatment applied to an artwork, distinct from the
original artwork and its meme profile.

**Enrichment run**:
A staged effort to collect and prepare an artist's artworks, images, descriptions
and matching material for review. Completing a run does not itself approve publication.

**Publication**:
Making reviewed catalogue material available to visitors. Publication is distinct
from discovering, generating or importing draft material.
